BASS BURST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how the Bass Burst game ("the game") handles information. The game is published by Veyroc Games. If you have any questions, contact us at info@veyrocgames.com.

What the game stores on your device

Your scores, best score, level, unlocked themes, records, settings, the number of days you have played and an unfinished game are saved only on your own device. We do not run servers and we never receive, collect or sell this information. Deleting the game deletes this data.

The game has no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA if you allow tracking), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ads.

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission before any tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is made for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Information stored by the game stays on your device until you delete the game. Information processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policy. You can also reset your advertising ID as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, and to object to or opt out of personalised advertising. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are handled by Google. You can still contact us and we will help.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not collect it ourselves. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob; where the law requires it, the game shows you a choice about personalised ads.

International processing

Google may process advertising information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under its own safeguards. We do not transfer any personal data ourselves.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The latest version is always available in the game and on this page, with the date of the last update.
