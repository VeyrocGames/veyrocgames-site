We keep things simple: our website collects nothing about you, and our games keep your progress on your own device.

01Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Veyroc Games handles information on this website (veyrocgames.com) and in our mobile games. Veyroc Games is the trading name of Burak Kayan, an individual developer based in Istanbul, Türkiye. For any question or request, contact info@veyrocgames.com.

02This website

This website has no user accounts, no contact forms, no analytics and no advertising. We do not set cookies.

If you choose a language, that choice is saved only in your own browser so the site opens in the same language next time. It is never sent to us.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when it delivers pages to you, under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to answer you.

03What our games store on your device

Your scores, best score, level, unlocked themes, records, settings, an unfinished game you can resume, and a count of the calendar days on which you have opened the game are stored only on your device, in the game's own local storage. The game has no server of its own. We do not receive, collect, sell or share this information.

The game also keeps, only on your device, a count of completed play sessions, the total time you have actively played, and whether the game has already asked you to rate it. These values are used only to decide whether the rating request may be shown, and it is shown at most once. They are never sent anywhere.

The day count is used only to decide when full-screen ads may be shown. It is never sent anywhere.

If you turn on Daily Reminders, the game stores your choice on your device and asks your phone to show up to three reminders a day at fixed local times (08:15, 12:15 and 19:45). These reminders are created on your device; no information about them is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

04Accounts, purchases and permissions

The game has no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. Its only use of the internet is loading ads from Google.

The game asks for notification permission only when you choose to enable Daily Reminders. If you decline, the game works normally.

05Ratings and reviews

The game may ask you once to rate it, using the rating window of Google Play or the App Store. You choose any rating yourself. The game does not see, store or read your rating or review, and it gives no reward or other benefit for rating. You can decline or ignore the request. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google or Apple under their own policies.

06Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as the advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA on iPhone and iPad when you allow tracking), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

07Your choices and consent

Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are requested. Your answer decides whether ads can be personalised.

If you decline personalised ads, Google can still show non-personalised ads. Where consent is required by law and not given, ads may not be shown at all. The game keeps working without ads; only the option to continue a game after an ad is then unavailable.

When Google requires it, a "Privacy & Ads" row appears in the game's Settings, where you can change your consent at any time.

08Android and iOS differences

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks once, the first time it starts, with Apple's standard tracking permission prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised based on tracking. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, the advertising ID is provided by Google Play services. You can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads; menu names vary by phone).

09Children

Our games are intended for adults aged 18 and over. The game is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the game removes it. On Android you can also remove it with Settings → Apps → (game name) → Storage → Clear data.

Data Google processes for advertising is kept under Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID as described above.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. The game holds no personal data about you on any server, so requests about advertising data are best made to Google through the links above. You can also contact us at info@veyrocgames.com and we will help where we can.

We do not sell your personal information.

12International processing

Google may process advertising information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under its own safeguards. We do not transfer any personal data ourselves.

13Changes

We may update this policy when the website or our games change. The latest version is always on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
