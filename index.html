About

A small studio with a big focus on craft.

Veyroc was founded in Istanbul to make mobile games that feel simple on the surface and deep underneath.

We handle the whole journey in-house: the idea, the game design, the art direction, the code, store launch and every update after it. Each game is built to run smoothly on a wide range of phones, released in many languages and supported for the long term.

How we work

Play it first. Then make it great.

Every game we ship goes through the same four steps. Ideas that are not fun in the first playable version don't move forward.

  1. Explore

    Idea

    We study what players love and look for a fresh twist on a proven mechanic.

  2. Prototype

    Playable

    Within days the idea becomes a playable build we can test and judge honestly.

  3. Polish

    Craft

    Art, sound, levels and feel are refined until every move feels satisfying.

  4. Launch

    Live

    Release on the App Store and Google Play, then listen, measure and improve.
